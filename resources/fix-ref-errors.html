How Do I Fix #REF! Errors in Excel?

Last Updated: June 2026

Quick Answer

A #REF! error means a formula is referring to a cell, row, column, or worksheet that no longer exists.

Most Common Causes

  • Deleted rows
  • Deleted columns
  • Deleted worksheets
  • Broken external references

Example

If this formula exists: 

=A1+B1

and column B is deleted, Excel may return: 

=A1+#REF!

How to Fix It

  1. Locate the missing reference.
  2. Restore the deleted row or column if possible.
  3. Update the formula to point to the correct location.
